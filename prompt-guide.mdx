## Overview

Storyfed processes videos sequentially by dividing them into individual **scenes**. A well-structured prompt provides both global instructions (affecting the entire video) and detailed, scene-by-scene directions. 

Writing clear, structured prompts ensures the AI accurately captures your creative vision, pacing, and visual style.

## Global Instructions

At the beginning of your prompt, you can define the overarching elements that apply to the entire video:

* **Visual Style:** Define the overall look (e.g., "3D Pixar style", "Cinematic dark fantasy", "Flat vector illustration"). You can also reinforce this by passing visual reference URLs in the `attachments` array of your API request.
* **Music Instructions:** Specify the mood or genre of the background track (e.g., "Upbeat lo-fi hip hop", "Tense cinematic strings").
* **Captions Style:** Detail how you want the subtitles to look (e.g., "Bold yellow text with black outlines, bouncing animation", "Minimalist white text at the bottom").
* **Visual References:** You can instruct the AI to use specific images attached in your API payload as character references or global style guides.

## Scene-by-Scene Breakdown

For the main content, break your script down into individual scenes. For each scene, you can specify the following parameters:

* **Type:** The media format for the scene (`image`, `video`, `narrator`, or `motion_graphics`).
* **Visual Instructions:** A detailed description of what is happening on screen.
* **On-Screen Text:** Text to display over the video. You can specify size, positioning (e.g., "Centered vertically and horizontally"), and animations.
* **On-Screen Floating Media:** Instructions for picture-in-picture elements, stickers, or overlays.
* **Transcript / Voiceover:** The exact words the AI voice should speak during this scene.
* **Length:** The estimated duration of the scene in seconds.

## Quality Tier Limitations

When specifying the `type` for your scenes, keep your requested **Quality Tier** in mind. Storyfed supports three tiers with different capabilities:

| Quality Tier | Supported Scene Types | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Budget** | `image`, `motion_graphics` | Perfect for explainer videos, news recaps, and stories using high-quality static assets and motion graphics. |
| **Standard** | `image`, `video`, `narrator`, `motion_graphics` | Unlocks dynamic video generation and AI narrator avatars. |
| **Pro** | `image`, `video`, `narrator`, `motion_graphics` | Highest fidelity rendering across all available media types. |

<Note>
If you submit a prompt requesting `video` or `narrator` scenes while using the **Budget** tier, the API will automatically downgrade those scenes to static images or motion graphics.
</Note>

## Example: Budget Tier Prompt

Here is a great example of a well-structured prompt designed for the **Budget** quality tier. It clearly separates the scenes, specifies the visual layout, and dictates the on-screen text and voiceover.

```text
[Global Instructions]
Style: Vibrant, highly detailed 2D digital art.
Captions: Dynamic bold white text with black drop shadow.
Music: Upbeat, curious electronic ambient.

[Topic: Unbelievable Life Facts]

[Scene 1]
Type: image
Visual: A striking static illustration of a person reacting to a smartphone screen.
On-Screen Text centered vertically and horizontally: "Things you didn't know 10 seconds ago"
Voiceover: "Here is a fact that sounds completely fake, but is 100 percent real."

[Scene 2]
Type: motion_graphics
Visual: Motion graphics animation that shows a brain and a light bulb side by side. Comparing how both use the same electricity.
On-Screen Text: "Your brain uses about 20 watts"
Voiceover: "Your brain uses roughly 20 watts of energy—about the power a small bulb draws. But it doesn't produce electricity you can use to light one."

[Scene 3]
Type: image
Visual: A static close-up illustration of a blinking eye, with a comment prompt overlay.
On-Screen Text: "Did you know this?"
Voiceover: "Comment if you already knew this, and follow for more surprising facts!"



## Example: Standard / Pro Tier Prompt (5 Scenes)

This example utilizes dynamic `video` generation and an AI `narrator` avatar to create an engaging, cinematic short-form documentary.

```text
[Global Instructions]
Style: Cinematic, hyper-realistic, documentary style.
Captions: Clean minimalist sans-serif text, bottom center, fade-in animation.
Music: Epic cinematic orchestral, building tension.

[Topic: The Future of Mars Exploration]

[Scene 1]
Type: narrator
Visual: Attached narrator in a modern tech studio looking directly into the camera.
On-Screen Text: "Next Stop: Mars" (Large, centered, elegant animation)
Voiceover: "Humanity is on the brink of its greatest adventure yet. But getting to Mars isn't the hardest part."
Length: 6 seconds

[Scene 2]
Type: video
Visual: A slow-motion, highly detailed video of a massive rocket lifting off from a launchpad at dawn.
On-Screen Text: "Launch is just step one."
Voiceover: "Escaping Earth's gravity requires millions of pounds of thrust and perfect precision."
Length: 5 seconds

[Scene 3]
Type: motion_graphics
Visual: A sleek 3D holographic map showing the orbits of Earth and Mars, with a dotted line connecting them.
On-Screen Text: "140 Million Miles Away" (Bottom left)
Voiceover: "The journey takes seven agonizing months through the deadly radiation of deep space."
Length: 6 seconds

[Scene 4]
Type: video
Visual: Sweeping drone-style video of a futuristic rover traversing the red, rocky Martian landscape.
On-Screen Floating Media: A small looping radar animation in the top right corner.
On-Screen Text: "Searching for life."
Voiceover: "Once there, our rovers scour the frozen deserts, hunting for ancient signs of life."
Length: 5 seconds

[Scene 5]
Type: narrator
Visual: The same professional AI narrator from Scene 1, smiling with an inspiring tone.
On-Screen Text: "Are you ready?"
Voiceover: "The countdown to Mars has already begun. Hit subscribe to follow the journey."
Length: 5 seconds


## Example: Highly Detailed Explainer (4 Scenes)

This Standard/Pro tier example showcases a comprehensive, educational explainer video. It perfectly blends all four media types—using a narrator for the hook, motion graphics for the technical explanation, stock images for conceptual visualization, and dynamic video for an inspiring outro. It also heavily utilizes floating media and precise text positioning.

```text
[Global Instructions]
Style: Premium educational explainer, modern 3D corporate-tech aesthetic, bright and engaging.
Captions: Clean Helvetica font, white text with a subtle dark blur behind it, word-by-word pop-up animation, bottom center.
Music: Upbeat, inquisitive tech-ambient track with a steady rhythm.

[Topic: The Science of Caffeine]

[Scene 1]
Type: narrator
Visual: The attached narrator dressed in a smart-casual blazer, standing in a brightly lit, modern laboratory setting, holding a cup of coffee, gesturing enthusiastically towards the camera.
On-Screen Text: "The Science of Coffee" (Large, bold, centered at the top, fades in/out).
Voiceover: "We all love our morning coffee, but have you ever wondered how this liquid gold actually keeps you awake?"
Length: 5 seconds

[Scene 2]
Type: motion_graphics
Visual: A smooth, high-quality 2D vector animation of the human brain. Zooming in, tiny glowing molecules labeled 'Adenosine' float towards nerve receptors, but suddenly bounce off as green 'Caffeine' molecules slide in to block the path.
On-Screen Text: "Caffeine Blocks Adenosine" (Positioned at the bottom center, just above the captions).
Voiceover: "Throughout the day, your brain produces adenosine, a chemical that makes you sleepy. Caffeine looks just like it, blocking those receptors so the tired signal never gets through."
Length: 8 seconds

[Scene 3]
Type: image
Visual: A high-quality image of an exhausted office worker slumped over their desk. The image has a slight blue color grade to convey lethargy and tiredness.
On-Screen Floating Media: Narrator face in a corner explaing.
On-Screen Text: "The Caffeine Crash" (Centered vertically and horizontally, large bold red font).
Voiceover: "But beware! Once the caffeine wears off, all that built-up adenosine rushes in at once, causing the dreaded afternoon crash."
Length: 6 seconds

[Scene 4]
Type: video
Visual: A cinematic, slow-motion 4K video of an energetic woman running up a set of city stairs at sunrise, looking highly focused, awake, and smiling.
On-Screen Text: "Use it wisely!" (Bottom center).
Voiceover: "So time your coffee right, stay hydrated, and use that energy boost to conquer your day. Hit subscribe for more bite-sized science!"
Length: 5 seconds
